About PLACA

The state agency for the HIV response

The Plateau State Agency for the Control of AIDS (PLACA) is the state body responsible for coordinating the multi-sectoral HIV and AIDS response. We work with line ministries, development partners, civil society, health facilities, Local Government AIDS Control Agencies (LACAs) and community groups.

Our work covers the whole state: all 17 Local Government Areas, urban and rural. We focus on prevention, testing, linkage to treatment, care and support, and the reliable reporting that helps decisions get made with evidence.

Our mandate

Coordinate the state HIV response, align partners around one plan, and report progress transparently to government and the public.

Our vision

A Plateau State with zero new HIV infections, zero AIDS-related deaths and zero stigma and discrimination.

Our mission

Use coordination, data and community partnership to expand prevention, treatment and care, especially for people most at risk.

What we do

Four ways we strengthen the response

PLACA does not deliver every service directly. We make the whole system work better by coordinating, supporting and tracking the organizations that do.

01

Coordination

Bring state ministries, partners and LACAs around shared priorities, workplans and referral pathways.

02

Data and reporting

Make reporting simple, catch inconsistent counts early, and turn approved reports into useful summaries.

03

Partner support

Help organizations plan, report and solve problems together, with clear guidance and paper-friendly tools.

04

Community engagement

Support awareness, reduce stigma and make sure community voices shape the response.

How reporting works

From a paper tally to a public figure

The platform is built around the way work is actually done: partners count on paper, enter totals when they have signal, and PLACA reviews before anything is published.

  1. 1

    Partners and LACAs submit counts

    Each organization reports aggregate numbers for its indicators for the period. No names or test results are collected.

  2. 2

    PLACA reviews and approves

    Officers check consistency, follow up on unusual numbers and approve the report. Rejected reports can be corrected and sent again.

  3. 3

    Approved totals are published

    Only approved, aggregated figures reach this page. The date of the latest update is always shown.
